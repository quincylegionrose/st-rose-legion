About Our Praesidium & History of the Legion

History of the Legion

The Legion of Mary had the most humble and spontaneous origins, arising without any premeditated plan, complex rules, or thought-out organization. A simple suggestion was thrown out, an evening was fixed, and a small group gathered together, entirely unaware that they were serving as instruments of a loving Providence.

The historic first enrolment of the legionaries took place at Myra House on Francis Street in Dublin, Ireland, at 8:00 p.m. on September 7, 1921, which happened to be the eve of the feast of Our Lady's Nativity. Although the date was determined by ordinary circumstances, years later it was viewed as a special touch of maternal love that the movement's birth coincided with the first fragrances of Our Lady's Nativity. Because of the title of the parent branch (Our Lady of Mercy), the organization was known for a time during its early years as "The Association of Our Lady of Mercy".

The first branch was formed through the collaboration of Frank Duff—a civil servant who at age 24 joined the Society of St. Vincent de Paul, acquiring a deep sensitivity to the underprivileged—alongside a group of Catholic women and Father Michael Toher, a priest of the Dublin Archdiocese.

The setting of that initial gathering became the permanent template for every Legion meeting worldwide. The table featured a simple altar centered around a statue of the Immaculate Conception (of the miraculous medal model) placed on a white cloth, flanked by two vases of flowers and two candlesticks with lighted candles.

The very first corporate act of those early legionaries was to kneel down, pray the invocation and prayer of the Holy Spirit, and slip through their fingers the beads of the simplest of all devotions—the Rosary. Under Mary's auspices, they discussed how best to please God and make Him loved, giving birth to the Legion in all its current features.

From September 7, 1921, until his death on November 7, 1980, at the age of 91, Frank Duff guided the worldwide extension of the Legion with heroic dedication. He later participated in the Second Vatican Council as a lay observer, and his Cause for Beatification was subsequently introduced in June 1996.

Membership & The Tessera

"This twofold auxiliary membership is to the Legion what its wings are to a bird. With these wings widely expanded by possession of many auxiliaries, and beating powerfully under the rhythmic drive of their faithful prayer, the Legion can soar into the higher air of supernatural ideal and effort."
— Official Legion of Mary Handbook
Active Member

Active Membership

  • Punctual attendance at the weekly Praesidium meeting (Sundays at 2:00 PM).
  • Daily recitation of the Catena Legionis and Tessera prayers.
  • Performance of weekly assigned apostolic work in union with the Praesidium.

Active Member Benefits

  • Holiness of the Member as the Primary Object
  • Growth in Christian Virtues Through an Ordered System
  • Deep Union with Mary and Transformation in Christ
  • Seeing and Serving Christ in Others
  • Strength and Guidance from the Weekly Meeting
Auxiliary Member

Auxiliary Membership

  • Serves as the spiritual prayer engine supporting active apostolic works.
  • Daily recitation of the Tessera prayers and full Rosary.

Auxiliary Member Benefits

  • Enormous Spiritual Return Through Mary
  • Multiplication and Purification of Prayers & Good Works
  • Renewed Purpose, Hope, and Sense of Mission
  • Helping All Souls & Sharing in the Legion's Treasury
Adjutorian Member

Adjutorian Membership

  • Designed for those unable to undertake the full duties of active or auxiliary membership due to illness, heavy responsibilities, or state of life.
  • Daily recitation of the full Legion prayer commitments (Tessera prayers and full Rosary) as a "veritable way of life."
  • Offers prayers and sufferings daily for the intentions of the Legion of Mary and the salvation of souls.

Adjutorian Member Benefits

  • Deeper Immersion in the Corporate Life and Prayer of the Church
  • A "Veritable Way of Life" and Expansion of Love
  • A Strong Spiritual Support for Souls and the Legion's Mission
  • All the Blessings of Auxiliary Membership Through Mary
